In this problem set, I analyzed a dataset of songs using SQL queries to extract various attributes such as danceability, energy, tempo, and song duration. Comparing songs by different artists like Drake and Post Malone showed clear differences in musical styles and audio characteristics. Working with JOINs and nested queries made it much easier to connect artist information with song details without making assumptions about database IDs. Overall, SQL provided an efficient way to query and filter complex audio data.
