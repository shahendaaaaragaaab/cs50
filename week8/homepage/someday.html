Someday

Someday, I want to write something of my own.

I always tell myself that I will start when I have more time, when my language is better, or when I finally feel ready.

But maybe waiting for the perfect moment is exactly what keeps me from starting.

You do not have to be perfect before you create something.

My goal

I want to improve my language, understand people better, and eventually turn my thoughts into stories.
