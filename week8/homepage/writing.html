Why I Like Writing

I like writing because it gives thoughts somewhere to exist. Sometimes I have an idea and keep thinking about it for a long time.

I am not a great writer yet, and my language is not perfect, but that does not mean I do not want to write.

What I want to write about

  • People and their personalities
  • Human behavior
  • Thoughts and emotions
  • Things I notice around me
